### Issues Encountered & Concrete Fixes

#### Issue 1: WebGL Hardware Attribute Limit Failure
- **Error**: `THREE.WebGLProgram: Shader Error 0 - VALIDATE_STATUS false. Program Info Log: Too many attributes (aInstanceDepthLayer)`.
- **Root Cause**: WebGL standard `MAX_VERTEX_ATTRIBS` is 16. Three.js `InstancedMesh` injects `attribute mat4 instanceMatrix` (4 attribute slots) plus `position` and `normal`. Together with 10 custom instanced attributes (`aInstanceTarget0..3`, `aInstanceRotAxis`, `aInstanceRotSpeed`, `aInstanceScale`, `aInstanceColorGroup`, `aInstancePhase`, `aInstanceDepthLayer`), the total reached 17 slots (> 16), failing shader linkage.
- **Resolution**: Packed the 6 scalar/vector attributes into two `vec4` attributes:
  - `attribute vec4 aInstanceRot;` (xyz: rotAxis, w: rotSpeed)
  - `attribute vec4 aInstanceParams;` (x: scale, y: colorGroup, z: phase, w: depthLayer)
  - Reduced total attribute slots to 13 (strictly <= 16).

#### Issue 2: Solid Plastic Shard Appearance vs Delicate Dala Wireframe Crystals
- **Root Cause**: Initial geometry generated solid, opaque triangular faces. In Dala, the shards are delicate, hollow wireframe prisms with high-specular glass interiors.
- **Resolution**:
  - Implemented hardware-level barycentric coordinates (`geom.setAttribute("aBarycentric", ...)`) on `createTetrahedralPyramidGeometry`.
  - Implemented Single-Pass Screen-Space Wireframe Rendering using `fwidth(vBarycentric)` in `DALA_FRAGMENT_SHADER`.
  - Wireframe edges render as razor-sharp, anti-aliased luminous lines (scale-independent), while facet interiors render as semi-transparent obsidian glass (`alpha = 0.04 - 0.45`) with Blinn-Phong specular gleams.

#### Issue 3: Asymmetric Negative Space & Hero Text Overlap
- **Root Cause**: `target0` generated a cylinder centered at $x = 0$, overlapping the left text column.
- **Resolution**:
  - Re-anchored `target0` vortex center to $x = +1.85$, $r \in [0.25, 1.80]$.
  - Left column ($x \in [-3.6, 0.0]$) is 100% clean obsidian negative space for the headline, subtitle, and CTAs.

  - Re-positioned mobile camera and world root ($y = -1.85, z = 8.2$) so the mobile headline sits in clean void above the vortex.

#### Issue 4: Control Deck on `/cinematic-prototype`
